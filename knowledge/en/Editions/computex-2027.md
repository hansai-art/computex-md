# COMPUTEX 2027: June 1-4, 2027, across three Taipei venues

COMPUTEX 2027 runs **June 1 to June 4, 2027**, four days, in Taipei. That is the schedule currently published on the show's official homepage.

Using three venues at once is the show's recent norm, not something specific to 2027.

## What has not been announced

The following were not on the official site as of the checked date, so this page leaves them blank rather than inferring them from aggregator sites or from what previous years did:

- Annual theme
- Exhibitor and booth counts
- Keynote speakers and agenda
- Zone layout and hall allocation
- Registration and ticketing dates

When the organizers publish these, this page gets updated and every checked date moves with it. If you want to help, open a pull request with a link to the official page. Figures without a source are not accepted.

## Why the page is shaped this way

Every page on COMPUTEX.md splits into two layers: a **fact layer** that is sourced and independently checkable, and a **curation layer** that carries editorial judgement. The table above is the fact layer. This section is curation.

The reason is practical. Trade show details get copied widely, and in the copying, "last year it was like this, so this year probably is too" turns into a flat assertion. Search engines and AI models then ingest it. By the time the organizers publish the real number, a wrong one is already circulating. We would rather leave the cell empty.
